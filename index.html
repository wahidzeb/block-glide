About Block Glide

Block Glide is a calm sliding puzzle. Chunky clay blocks sit on a small board, and coloured gates wait on the walls. Slide each block out through a gate of its own colour until the board is empty.

Later chapters add special blocks such as ice, and there are boosters, a Daily Challenge and colour-blind symbols that can be turned on in Settings.

3 quick tips

  1. Look for the block that is in everyone's way, and move it first.
  2. Count gate widths. A gate two squares wide only takes blocks that are one or two squares across on that side.
  3. On timed levels, plan before you touch anything. The clock waits for your first move.
